## Safety and Side Effects: What to Expect

While retinol is effective, it can cause side effects such as mild redness, tingling, and flaking, especially when first introduced to the skin. These effects are generally temporary and indicate that the product is working. To minimize side effects, it's advisable to start with a lower concentration and gradually increase usage as your skin builds tolerance. Neutrogena's products are formulated to be gentle, but individual tolerance can vary. Using a moisturizer after applying retinol can help soothe the skin and reduce dryness. If irritation persists, consult a dermatologist for personalized advice. It's also crucial to use sunscreen daily, as retinol can increase skin sensitivity to sunlight.

## Long-term Effects of Retinol Use

Long-term use of retinol can lead to sustained improvements in skin health. Retinol is often compared to other anti-aging ingredients like peptides and antioxidants.
