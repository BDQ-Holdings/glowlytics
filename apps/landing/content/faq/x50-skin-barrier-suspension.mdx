## Understanding Skin Barrier Function

The skin barrier, also known as the stratum corneum, is the outermost layer of the skin that serves as a protective shield. It comprises dead skin cells and a lipid matrix that prevents moisture loss and shields against environmental damage. This barrier is crucial for maintaining skin hydration and protecting against irritants, pathogens, and allergens. According to research in "Skin Pharmacology and Physiology," the integrity of the skin barrier is vital for skin health, as it regulates moisture retention and prevents external damage.

## How can I tell if my skin barrier is damaged?

A damaged skin barrier often manifests through symptoms such as dryness, redness, itching, and increased sensitivity. When your skin barrier is compromised, it may also lead to conditions like eczema or acne flare-ups due to the skin's inability to retain moisture and protect against irritants. If you notice persistent symptoms, it is advisable to consult a dermatologist for a comprehensive evaluation and guidance.
