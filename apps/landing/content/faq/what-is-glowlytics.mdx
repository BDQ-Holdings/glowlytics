Glowlytics is an AI-powered skin health tracking app built by doctors. It uses your smartphone camera to analyze five key skin signals — inflammation, hydration, sun damage, elasticity, and structure — and tracks them over time so you can see what's working and what isn't.

## How Does It Work?

Each day you take a quick scan with the Glowlytics camera. Our three-layer vision system analyzes your skin in seconds, giving you a score for each signal. Over time you build a picture of how your skin responds to products, sleep, stress, diet, and environment.

## Who Is It For?

Glowlytics is for anyone who wants to take a data-driven approach to their skin health — whether you're managing acne, fighting signs of aging, or just curious about what actually moves the needle.
