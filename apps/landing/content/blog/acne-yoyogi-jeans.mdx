### Softness

Soft fabrics reduce friction against the skin. The Yoyogi jeans' soft denim twill is a good example of a fabric that minimizes irritation. Look for materials that feel gentle against your skin to prevent chafing and irritation.

### Fit

A looser fit can reduce pressure and friction on the skin. While the wide-leg design of the Yoyogi jeans is beneficial, ensure that the waist and hip areas are not too tight. A comfortable fit allows for better air circulation and reduces the risk of acne mechanica.
