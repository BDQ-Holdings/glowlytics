## Understanding Yeast Infections and Their Causes

Imagine this: you're going about your daily routine, only to be interrupted by an uncomfortable itch or irritation. Many people have experienced this scenario, often caused by a common culprit—yeast infections. These infections are not only uncomfortable but can also be recurrent, making them a significant concern for those with sensitive skin. Yeast infections, medically known as candidiasis, are caused by an overgrowth of a fungus called Candida. While Candida is naturally present in the body, an imbalance can lead to infection. Factors contributing to this imbalance include hormonal changes, antibiotics, and even the use of certain soaps and personal care products. According to [Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/yeast-infection/symptoms-causes/syc-20378999), these infections are characterized by itching, redness, and a thick, white discharge.

### What Causes Yeast Infections?

Yeast infections occur when the balance of bacteria and yeast in the vagina is disrupted. This can happen due to several reasons:

- **Antibiotics**: These can kill beneficial bacteria that keep yeast in check.
- **Hormonal Changes**: Pregnancy, menopause, and birth control pills can alter hormone levels, affecting yeast growth.
- **Diabetes**: High blood sugar levels can promote yeast growth.
- **Immune System Disorders**: A weakened immune system can lead to more frequent infections.
- **Personal Care Products**: Harsh soaps and scented products can disrupt the natural pH balance.
