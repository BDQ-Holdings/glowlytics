### The Role of Lipids and Peptides in Skin Health

Lipids and peptides are fundamental components of a healthy skin barrier. Lipids, including ceramides, cholesterol, and fatty acids, form the mortar that holds skin cells together, preventing moisture loss and protecting against environmental aggressors. Peptides, on the other hand, are short chains of amino acids that act as building blocks for proteins such as collagen and elastin, essential for skin strength and elasticity.

Scientific studies have shown that a combination of lipids and peptides can significantly enhance skin barrier function. ## Detailed Explanation: How Peptides and Ceramides Work Together
