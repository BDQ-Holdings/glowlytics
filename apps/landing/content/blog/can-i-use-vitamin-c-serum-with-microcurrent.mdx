## Compatibility of Vitamin C with Microcurrent Devices

The compatibility of Vitamin C serums with microcurrent devices largely depends on the formulation of the Vitamin C used. Certain forms, like L-ascorbic acid, are known to oxidize quickly, which can lead to irritation when combined with microcurrent treatments. This is because oxidation can reduce the efficacy of Vitamin C and potentially cause skin reactions [source](https://myotone.com/what-serums-not-to-use-with-a-microcurrent-device/).

### Safe Alternatives to L-Ascorbic Acid

To safely use Vitamin C with microcurrent devices, consider stabilized derivatives such as sodium ascorbyl phosphate. These alternatives are less likely to oxidize and can provide the same antioxidant benefits without the risk of irritation. They are also more stable in formulations, ensuring that the Vitamin C remains effective throughout its use [source](https://myotone.com/what-serums-not-to-use-with-a-microcurrent-device/).
