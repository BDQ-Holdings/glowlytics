### Understanding DPI and Its Importance in Dermatology

DPI, or dots per inch, is a measure of spatial printing or video dot density. In the context of dermatology, a higher DPI allows for finer detail in printed images, which is essential for accurately capturing the intricacies of skin texture and tone.
