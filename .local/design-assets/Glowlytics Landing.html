— 03

What we watch.
Just four things.

Skin is impossibly complex. We don't pretend otherwise. But four well-measured facets, repeated daily, will tell you almost everything you need to know about what's helping and what isn't.

Hydrated

Surface plumpness · 0–100

How dewy and full your skin sits today. Most sensitive to sleep, water, and the moisture step you may have skipped last night.

Measured across forehead, cheek, jawline · 14 ref. points

Calm

Redness map · week-over-week

Distribution and intensity of warm tones. We're not chasing zero — we're chasing your baseline, and noticing when something pushed you off it.

Heat-mapped by zone · trigger correlation built in

Even

Tone variance + post-mark fade

How uniform your tone is, and how quickly old marks are leaving. The slowest of the four to move — and the most rewarding when it does.

L*a*b* delta · 28-day rolling fade index

Firm

Micro-bounce · cheek + jaw

How quickly your skin returns from a smile. A long-arc signal — meaningful over months, not days. We mark it but never alarm you.

Time-resolved elastography from a single capture

— 10

Honest answers
to honest questions.

The things people ask us at dinner parties. If yours isn't here, we'll happily answer it — write us, the address is in the footer.
Does it work on every skin tone?+
Yes. Our reference set spans Fitzpatrick I through VI, balanced across age and condition. Hydration and calm are tone-invariant; tone variance and post-mark fade are calibrated against your own baseline rather than a universal target, so the app never holds you to someone else's skin.
How long until I see patterns?+
Three to four weeks for the first strong pattern. We could surface them sooner — most apps do — but they wouldn't be true. Skin moves on weeks, and we'd rather be slow and right than fast and noisy.
What if I miss a day, or a week?+
Nothing happens. We don't punish gaps, scold them, or design the streak to make you feel terrible. The patterns just take a little longer to surface, and we'll let you know when they're back.
Is it free?+
Free to start, forever. The core daily read, weekly patterns, and full shelf verdicts are included. Glow+ ($6/mo or $48/yr) unlocks deep history (beyond 90 days), full timeline exports, and side-by-side compare. We don't take a cent from brands.
iOS only?+
iOS 16+ at launch — the readings rely on the TrueDepth front-facing camera and on-device Neural Engine. A read-only web mirror for your timeline is in private beta. Android is on the roadmap once on-device depth is more uniform.
What about lighting? Doesn't it ruin the readings?+
We normalize against soft natural light from the side — the kind a window gives you. The app gently coaches you toward the right setup the first few times, and quietly tags any reading taken in low or harsh light as lower-confidence in your timeline.
