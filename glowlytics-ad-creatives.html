02. Instagram / TikTok Ad Scripts

Short-form video scripts (15–30 seconds each) optimized for social media feeds. Each includes hook, body, CTA, and visual direction.

Script 1: POV: Doctor Edition
Duration
15 seconds
Hook (0–3s)
"I spent 8 years in medical school to help you. So I built something better."
Body (3–11s)
Cut to phone mockup showing a 30-second scan. Real-time lesion detection overlay. AI analyzing skin in teal glow. Results card appears with hydration, elasticity, structure scores. Doctor's voice: "Every scan trains an AI on AAD guidelines. Not Instagram trends. Actual science."
CTA (11–15s)
"Get your first scan free. Link in bio." [Button appears: "Try Free"]
Visual Direction
Start with doctor in white coat (candid, friendly). Cut to product demo on phone. Slow-mo scanning animation. Teal accents and glow effects. End on results screen with button. Music: upbeat, modern, trustworthy.
Script 2: 30-Day Results
Duration
30 seconds
Hook (0–3s)
"I scanned my face every day for 30 days. Here's what happened to my skin."
Body (3–23s)
Time-lapse montage: Day 1 hydration 42% → Day 7 hydration 56% → Day 14 elasticity improves → Day 30 full report card glowing. Show phone in hand doing quick scans. Text overlays: "Changed products week 2" / "Added SPF week 3" / "Consistency matters." Real skin transformation visible across frames. Voice: "The app tracked every change. No guessing. No lies. Just real progress."
CTA (23–30s)
"Track yours free for 7 days. See the difference." [App Store badge]
Visual Direction
Influencer/relatable user doing quick daily scans. Calendar days flip in background. Phone shows metric improvements in real time (hydration bar filling, elasticity rising). Green checkmarks appear for consistency streaks. Uplifting music. Bright, energetic cuts. Natural lighting.
Script 3: Problem-Agitate-Solve
Duration
20 seconds
Hook (0–3s)
"Your skincare routine might be hurting you. Here's how to know."
Body (3–16s)
[PROBLEM] Show frustrated user looking in mirror. TikTok text: "Spending $200/month on products." [AGITATE] "But no real results. Just hope." Show empty skincare bottles, confused scrolling through reviews. [SOLVE] "What if your skin could tell you what actually works?" Cut to Glowlytics app. Phone camera scans face. Results appear: "Hydration down 20% this week. Switch your moisturizer." Real data, real answers. Voice: "Stop guessing. Start knowing."
CTA (16–20s)
"Glowlytics. Free 7-day trial. Swipe up." [CTA button]
Visual Direction
Relatable frustration at start (mirror, confused face). Quick cuts showing skincare overwhelm (bottles, ads, confusion). Transition to app demo with satisfying "ding" and glow effect. Show AI analysis happening (teal particles, scanning animation). End on confident, informed user smiling at results. Music: start anxious/busy, resolve to calm, empowering.
